A Life by His Design

Allah created each of us with a purpose, and humanity took on the Trust (al-Amānah) — the responsibility of living in accordance with His guidance.

A life is not lived all at once. It is lived in ordinary days — in how we pray, work, love, raise families, respond to hardship, spend, rest, serve and choose. A Life by His Design asks what that purpose looks like on an ordinary Tuesday, for a woman like you living in the modern world.

Islam continually brings us back to the day in front of us: to prayer, remembrance, repentance, intention and the reality that tomorrow is never guaranteed. What we repeatedly do with our days is shaping the life we will one day return to Allah with.

Indeed, Allah does not change the condition of a people until they change what is within themselves.

Surah Ar-Ra'd 13:11

Among the du'as the Prophet ﷺ made most frequently was: “O Turner of hearts, keep my heart firm upon Your deen.”

That is part of what these two days of retreat and conference are for: returning to the Qur'an and Sunnah, seeking knowledge, remembering what we forget, and renewing what keeps the heart anchored to Allah, so that what changes within us can shape how we live.

The ME stands for Muslimah Empowered

Being ME is a registered Canadian non-profit working to connect Muslim women and girls in faith to ignite change. It began as a single conference in 2011 and now runs a portfolio of events built around faith, confidence, action and community.

The conference exists to give Muslim women an outlet to speak about the reality of their lives and the work they do that benefits others — becoming pillars that support each other and the deen.
