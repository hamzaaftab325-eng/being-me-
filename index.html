How you live your day is how you live your life.

An invitation to live on purpose

Allah created us with a purpose and entrusted us with the responsibility to live by His guidance.

A Life by His Design asks what that purpose looks like on an ordinary Tuesday — in how you pray, work, raise children, spend, rest and choose. For two days we return to the Qur'an and Sunnah, not as ideas to admire, but as a design for the day in front of you.

Come with the year you have actually been living, and leave with a plan for the next one.

The ME stands for Muslimah Empowered

Being ME is a registered Canadian non-profit working to connect Muslim women and girls in faith to ignite change. It began as a single conference in 2011 and now runs a portfolio of events built around faith, confidence, action and community.

The conference exists to give Muslim women an outlet to speak about the reality of their lives and the work they do that benefits others — becoming pillars that support each other and the deen.
